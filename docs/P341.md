## RiskActionGuard Binding Boundary

RiskActionGuard source binding is the future review-only action-review evidence layer after MultiTimeframeContext and before WatchlistPoolProof / source-owned candidate integration.

RiskActionGuard is an action review layer, not an execution layer.

It may carry only explicit input fields:

- SourceTrace refs;
- RuntimeKlineContext ref;
- DataQualityContext ref;
- MultiTimeframeContext ref;
- primary timeframe;
- liquidity state and degraded flag;
- stampede / wick-only / multi-timeframe confirmation / strong reversal flags;
- risk level;
- risk score;
- action risk score;
- proposed review-only action label;
- allowed review-only action labels;
- blocked action labels;
- guard decision label;
- guard reason;
- risk action category;
- risk action boundary ref;
- missing / degraded / blocked reasons.

It does not calculate risk. It does not read market data. It does not decide final direction. It does not generate actions. It does not generate point values.

## Core Safety Rules

P341 fixes these RiskActionGuard semantics as review-only validation rules:

- high risk is not immediate stop, immediate reverse, or immediate open;
- strong reversal is not direct reverse;
- wick-only is not trend reversal;
- stampede state blocks opportunity push, reverse, and new open semantics;
- high risk with normal liquidity can only become review-only degraded context, not an execution command;
- high risk with degraded liquidity cannot become one-shot market cut semantics and must remain fail-closed / degraded / manual review;
- high risk with stampede must be `BLOCKED_FAIL_CLOSED`;
- high risk with only short-term wick evidence must remain `INCOMPLETE` or degraded until confirmation exists.
