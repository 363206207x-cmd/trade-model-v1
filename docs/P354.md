## Safety And Factory Rules

The DTO factories force:

- `reviewOnly=true`;
- `notTradeInstruction=true`;
- `manualReviewRequired=true`;
- `incompleteSafe=true`;
- `failClosed=true` only when status is `BLOCKED_FAIL_CLOSED`.

Factory rules:

- `incomplete(...)` requires missingReason or candidateUnavailableReason.
- `blockedFailClosed(...)` requires blockedReason or candidateBlockedReason.
- `degraded(...)` requires candidateDegradedReason or degradedReasons.
- `reviewOnly(...)` preserves review-only safety flags.
- list fields are defensively copied.
- no factory outputs executable point, direction, or action fields.
